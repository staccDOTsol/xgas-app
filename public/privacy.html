Privacy Policy

Effective: September 22, 2026 · Operator of xgas.dev · Contact: jarettrsdunn+meta@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes how the operators of xgas.dev (“we”, “us”) handle information when you use the website, the hosted Model Context Protocol (MCP) endpoint at xgas.dev/mcp, documentation, and related interfaces (together, the “Services”).

1. What we are

xGas is a noncustodial interface and tool layer over on-chain contracts (vault bridge, P2P OTC desk, NGU curves, and related protocols). We do not take custody of your assets in the ordinary prepare-and-sign flow: tools quote and prepare; your wallet signs. Optional agent wallets (for example via Privy after X sign-in) are described separately below.

2. Information we collect

  • Wallet and chain data. Public blockchain addresses, transaction hashes, and on-chain state you ask the Services to read or prepare. This data is already public on the relevant networks.
  • Account / auth data (if you sign in). If you use Sign in with X, we may receive identifiers and profile fields X provides, and may associate a Privy-managed wallet with that session for agent use. That path is optional.
  • Linked accounts via Plaid (optional). If you choose to check a desk payment with Plaid, you link an account through Plaid Inc. and we receive read-only access to that account's names, last four digits and recent transactions (about 30 days). We use it only to look for the payment of the trade you ask about. We store Plaid's access token encrypted, never your bank login, and keep a one-way fingerprint of a matched transaction so it cannot be counted for two trades. We do not keep your transaction history. If you publish a Plaid receipt to a dispute, the matched transaction's date, amount, description and account last four digits become public. Unlinking removes the connection at Plaid and here. Plaid's handling of your data is governed by the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.
  • Technical logs. IP address, user agent, timestamps, request paths, and error logs needed to operate, secure, and debug the Services.
  • Communications. Email or messages you send us (including the support address above).
  • What we do not collect by design. We do not ask for seed phrases or private keys. We do not process card payments or hold fiat as a platform. On/off ramps available through the stack are peer-to-peer between users.

3. How we use information

  • To operate the website, MCP tools, RPC/proxy routes, and related infrastructure.
  • To prepare unsigned transactions and relay signed ones when you (or your authorized agent) request it.
  • To secure the Services, prevent abuse, and comply with law where applicable.
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Peer-to-peer activity and disputes

Fiat-facing or off-chain settlement legs that users arrange through the desk or ramps are strictly peer-to-peer. Dispute handling is designed to be resolved among users through the protocol’s mechanisms, without platform interference in the commercial outcome between counterparties. We are not a bank, escrow agent for fiat, or payment processor for those P2P legs.

5. Sharing

We may share information with infrastructure providers strictly as needed to host the Services (for example cloud hosting, auth/wallet infrastructure such as Privy or X OAuth, Plaid for accounts you choose to link, and analytics-free operational logging). We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety, integrity, or rights of users or the Services.

6. On-chain permanence

Transactions you broadcast are public and generally irreversible. Deleting an account with us (if any) does not erase blockchain history.

7. Retention

Operational logs are retained as long as reasonably needed for security, debugging, and legal obligations. Auth session data lasts for the life of the session or token you authorize.

Plaid data. Plaid access tokens and the names and last four digits of linked accounts are kept until you unlink; unlinking deletes them here and removes the connection at Plaid immediately. Transactions are read at the moment you ask for a check and are not stored. We keep a one-way fingerprint of a transaction matched to a trade, which cannot be turned back into the transaction, so it can not be counted for two trades. A Plaid receipt you publish to a dispute is public by your choice and is kept as long as the dispute record exists. To have everything tied to your X account deleted, email the support address above; we act on it within 30 days. We review this retention schedule at least every six months and when we change what we collect.

8. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 18. Do not use them if you are under 18.

9. International users

The Services may be hosted in multiple regions. By using them you understand your information may be processed where our providers operate.
